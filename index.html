The voice behind the mission

Dr. G.V. Ramakrishna Murthy

Founding Mentor · Scientist turned spiritual teacher

As a child he learnt the Bala Ramayanam, Shabda Manjari and Bhagavad Gita from his father. As an engineer he spent his career at the Nuclear Fuel Complex, Department of Atomic Energy. Today he explains idol worship, daily ritual and scripture through scientific reasoning that students, homemakers and seniors can all follow.

    Since 2005

    A legacy of initiatives

    InitiativeTeertha Yatra
    1. 2005

      Bhagavad Gita classes

      "Bhagavad Gita Nerchukundam" began for children at Bhakta Sanjeevani Devalayam, NFC, with free Gitas for every child.

    2. 2006

      Ashtanga Yoga & Surya Namaskaras

      Free dawn sessions across Dr. A.S. Rao Nagar and ECIL, with a mass gathering every International Yoga Day.

    3. 2006 – 2013

      Educational institutions

      Secretary of Saraswathi Sishu Mandir, Kushaiguda, and leadership at Sri Sharada Institute and Sri Vidya Vihar.

    4. 2011

      Bhakti Jnana Prabodhanacharya

      Honoured with the title for a decade of making Dharma understandable.

    5. 2019

      Dharma Chaitanya Vedika

      An online platform with daily classes, Monday to Saturday, for homemakers and seekers across India.

    The vision

    An ashram where the Indian way of life is lived, not just taught.

    Guruji's life goal: a home for discourse, yoga, Goshala, bhajan and children's learning under one banyan tree. Every class you attend and every hour you give brings it closer.
