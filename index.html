Dharma, explained scientifically, for every generation.

For 25 years, Sri Haritha Dharma Parishad has carried scripture, ritual and self-reliant living to students, homemakers and seekers, in a language this generation understands.

Shloka of the day

विद्या विनयेन शोभते

Vidyā vinayena śobhate

Learning shines through humility. What we learn should show in how we act.

      The voice behind the mission

      Dr. Gaddamanugu Venkata Ramakrishna Murthy

      Founding Mentor · Scientist turned spiritual teacher

      As a child he learnt the Bala Ramayanam, Shabda Manjari and Bhagavad Gita from his father. As an engineer he spent his career at the Nuclear Fuel Complex, Department of Atomic Energy. Today he explains idol worship, daily ritual and scripture through scientific reasoning that students, homemakers and seniors can all follow.

        A legacy of initiatives

          Jagadguru Shankaracharya Sri Sri Swayamprakasha Sachidananda Saraswathi Mahaswamiji

          Sri Adi Shankaracharya Sharada Lakshminarasimha Peetam, Hariharapura

          Guruji's work continues under the guidance of the Peetam, where rigorous Vedic ritual and progressive community outreach go together. Spiritual awakening and social equality, hand in hand.

          The vision

          An ashram where the Indian way of life is lived, not just taught.

          Guruji's life goal: a home for discourse, yoga, Goshala, bhajan and children's learning under one banyan tree. Every class you attend and every hour you give brings it closer.
